First Internship - Groupe SIM

This internship at SIM Group gave me a clear and practical understanding of industrial automation and its role in modern production. I applied the theoretical knowledge from university to real industrial tasks and learned about safety, quality, and environmental protection through QHSE training.
I gained hands-on experience with PLCs, SCADA systems, HMI interfaces, and the stages of automation projects, including wiring, programming, and testing. Studying electric motors and control methods helped me understand how to choose suitable technical and energy solutions.
Analyzing a submersible pump wiring and observing the couscous production process showed me the importance of automation for improving performance, reliability, and quality. Using discrete event systems and Zelio Soft software further strengthened my understanding of control systems.
Overall, this internship was a valuable experience that enhanced my technical skills, deepened my interest in industrial automation, and prepared me for my future professional career.
